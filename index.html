Our mission

To turn protein dynamics into selective, validated therapeutic opportunities — revealing hidden regulatory sites and designing interventions that act only where they should.

Protein motion

Proteins are machines in motion.

A crystal structure is a single snapshot. AllotarX™ samples the full conformational ensemble at ~1000× the speed of conventional molecular dynamics, so we see how a target really moves.

Hidden regulatory sites

As the protein moves, hidden pockets open.

Interpretable AI ranks these transient allosteric sites by druggability, dynamics and selectivity signatures, with 90% benchmarked accuracy.

Selectivity first

One site, one protein, one precise intervention.

We design site-specific modalities — small molecules, macrocyclic peptides and designed scaffolds — and test every one for binding, function and selectivity.

02 Our approach

Three ideas behind every Allotar program

Physics-driven

Physics-based hybrid simulation across nanosecond to millisecond timescales maps how each target protein moves.

AI-guided

Interpretable AI finds allosteric sites and selectivity signatures across the whole conformational landscape.

Lab-in-the-loop

Functional validation with partner laboratories feeds every result, hit or miss, back into the next design cycle.

03 AllotarX™ workflow

From dynamic ensembles to selective therapeutics

Identify functional allosteric sites, prioritize druggable pockets, design selective interventions, and improve every cycle with functional validation.

Design

Functional allosteric sites mapped across dynamic conformational ensembles — selective interventions designed across small molecules, macrocyclic peptides, and computationally designed scaffolds

Build

Ensemble-guided library construction — de novo macrocyclic peptide design and focused screening against dynamic regulatory sites

Test

Functional validation with partner laboratories — biochemical and cell-based assays confirming mechanism-level activity

Learn

Every outcome — hit or miss — sharpens the model. ADME-enriched data feeds sharper priors into the next Design cycle
