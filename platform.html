AllotarX™ platform

From Protein Dynamics to Precision Allosteric Discovery

AllotarX™ transforms protein conformational ensembles into actionable, selective allosteric sites—combining physics-based simulation, interpretable AI, and closed-loop validation to design safer, more precise therapeutics.

SCROLL

Start with the site, not the molecule. Most discovery begins from a pocket that is already known. We begin one step earlier — asking which site actually controls function, and whether it is distinctive enough to act on without touching related proteins. That choice shapes everything that follows.

02 Dynamics sampling

Ultra-Fast Dynamics Sampling

Maps protein conformational landscapes at ~1000× speed

Enables end-to-end, ensemble-based drug discovery workflow.

Impact
  • Unlocks target dynamics and key transition states for precision allosteric control
  • Reveals cryptic sites and mechanisms underlying protein function and modulation
  • Increases the number of functionally relevant hits
  • Scales to large multi-chain complexes and assemblies that conventional MD cannot sample long enough to reveal functional motions.

03 Allosteric discovery

Allosteric Discovery with Selectivity-First Design

Identifies allosteric sites across protein conformational landscapes with 90% benchmarked accuracy.

Introduces target selectivity from the earliest stages of discovery through interpretable AI leveraging druggability, dynamics, and allosteric signatures.

Impact
  • Unlocks previously undruggable targets to enable precise functional control.
  • Delivers selective modulators with reduced off-target effects and improved safety.
  • Lowers attrition risk in late-stage optimization and clinical development.
04 Closed-loop discovery

Closed-Loop, Multi-Site and Multi-Modality Discovery

Designs site-specific modalities — small molecules, macrocyclic peptides, computationally designed scaffolds — tailored to the dynamic and geometric features of each regulatory site.

Integrates experimental data on binding, function, and selectivity to continuously refine predictions and guide design.

Impact
  • Reduces optimization cycles and timelines
  • Increases the fraction of compounds achieving desired functional activity and selectivity
  • Enhances functional outcomes through modality-specific design
