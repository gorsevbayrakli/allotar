Programs

From allosteric insight to selective medicines

Our programs apply AllotarX™ to proteins whose function depends on dynamics — targets where static, structure-based approaches have struggled. We begin where selectivity matters most, in neurodegeneration, and are expanding across therapeutic areas including oncology and immunology.

01 Our approach

Advancing focused therapeutic programs

Allotar’s programs target therapeutic areas where dynamic protein modeling and allosteric control can unlock new treatment strategies. By integrating physics-based simulation with machine learning, the platform enables faster identification and refinement of high-quality drug candidates.

01

Prioritize High-Impact Targets

We focus on disease areas where traditional approaches have struggled, identifying proteins with dynamic regulatory mechanisms that offer new intervention opportunities.

02

Discover Novel Control Sites

Our platform reveals hidden allosteric sites and transient conformations, enabling access to targets and mechanisms not addressable through static structural models.

03

Advance Selective Candidates

We design and optimize highly selective modulators, progressing candidates with improved efficacy and reduced off-target risk toward experimental validation.
