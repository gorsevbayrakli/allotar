Programs

From allosteric mechanisms to selective medicines

Advancing programs in neurodegeneration and expanding into therapeutic areas including oncology and pain. We focus on targets where dynamic regulation and selectivity create opportunities for differentiated therapeutic intervention.

Our approach

Advancing focused therapeutic programs

AllotarX™ accelerates the identification and refinement of therapeutic candidates by integrating physics-based, AI-driven approaches with closed-loop learning.

High-Impact Targets

We focus on targets governed by dynamic regulatory mechanisms, where traditional structure-based approaches have struggled and allosteric control can create new intervention opportunities.

New Interventions

Working from conformational ensembles, we uncover allosteric mechanisms, including transient regulatory sites and states, that static structural models do not reveal.

Selective Candidates

We design and optimize selective modulators, refining candidates against related proteins through experimental feedback and closed-loop learning.
